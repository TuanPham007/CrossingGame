CROSSING GAME - BAN HOAN THIEN KIEN TRUC + MAP LAP HAZARD/SAFE
==============================================================

MUC TIEU CUA BAN NAY
-------------------
Ban nay duoc phat trien tu code moi nhat cua nhom va tap trung vao 2 muc tieu:
1) Bo phu thuoc gameplay vao background.PNG/background2.png de nhom tu thiet ke lai giao dien sau.
2) Bo sung cac subsystem con thieu khi doi chieu voi Group 5/6, nhung khong lam game qua phuc tap de demo.

1. MAP GAMEPLAY MOI: 1 HAZARD + 1 SAFE LAP LAI
----------------------------------------------
Trong main.cpp:
- LANE_STEP = 100 px
- HAZARD_REPEAT = 200 px
- Vi tri xuat phat cua player la SAFE lane.
- Cac hazard lane nam cach nhau 200 px.
- O giua 2 hazard lane luon co 1 safe lane.
- Obstacle/river/train chi duoc tao tren hazard row.
- Safe row hien tai to mau xanh la de nhom de nhin va thiet ke lai.

Ham quan trong:
    drawSimpleLaneMap(...)

Ham nay chi ve placeholder:
- hazard = xam
- safe = xanh la

Sau nay muon dung hinh anh, nhom chi can thay phan ve RectangleShape trong
`drawSimpleLaneMap()` bang 2 texture tile:
    hazardTile.png
    safeTile.png
KHONG can sua collision, spawn hay camera.

background.PNG va background2.png van de trong thu muc de khong lam mat asset cu,
nhung gameplay core khong con dung 2 file nay.

4. MULTITHREADING AN TOAN
-------------------------
File moi:
    GameLogicWorker.h
    GameLogicWorker.cpp

Worker su dung std::jthread + std::atomic.
Worker KHONG truy cap sprite, vector obstacle hay RenderWindow.
No chi xu ly logic timing cua traffic light va logic tick.

Main Thread:
    SFML event
    player input
    world update
    rendering

Worker Thread:
    traffic phase timer
    logic tick

Cach tach nay tranh race condition trong khi van the hien multithreading ro rang.
