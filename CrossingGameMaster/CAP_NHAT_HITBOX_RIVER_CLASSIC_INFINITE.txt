CROSSING GAME - CAP NHAT HITBOX / RIVER / CLASSIC / INFINITE
=============================================================

1) HITBOX
- CPEOPLE.cpp: player scale 0.20 -> 0.19; hitbox theo phan than; vien DO.
- CCAR.cpp / CBIRD.cpp / CWHALE.cpp: can lai hitbox theo vung anh that.
- CVEHICLE.cpp / CANIMAL.cpp: vien hitbox VANG.
- F3: bat/tat hien thi hitbox. Ban nay de ON mac dinh de de test.

2) RIVER / DEEP RIVER
- CWaterLane.h/.cpp: vung nuoc gay chet thu hep con 50px de khong an vao dai dat giua 2 cum song.
- Chi chet khi hitbox player thuc su cham WATER va khong co LOG support.
- Khi roi nuoc, player respawn tren log dang hien tren lane river gan nhat.
- Them safe land mau xanh giua lane river thu 2 va thu 3 cua cac cum river lien tiep.

3) CLASSIC MODE
- 10 stage.
- Stage 1-4: khong co river.
- River bat dau tu Stage 5 tro di (Stage 5 Mixed, Stage 6-7 Water, Stage 8-10 co the mix river).
- Dieu kien clear: Score >= 1000 * Stage.
  Stage 1: 1000
  Stage 2: 2000
  ...
  Stage 10: 10000
- Lane Classic duoc sinh tiep theo camera, khong bi het obstacle truoc khi du diem.

4) INFINITE MODE
- Lane dau / khu player bat dau dung la safe lane, khong spawn obstacle tren do.
- Difficulty tang moi 200 diem (+10% speed).
- River chi co the bat dau spawn sau 1000 diem.
- River spawn theo cum 3 lane; giua lane 2 va 3 co safe land mau xanh.
